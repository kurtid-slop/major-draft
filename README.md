# Major Draft

A CS Major lineup game in the spirit of 82-0. Each round spins a Major and one of its teams, and you sign one player from that team. After five rounds your lineup is placed against every real Major team from 2013 to 2026, based on how each player's K/D compared with the average team at their own Major.

- **Normal mode** shows each player's K/D, rating, K−D and maps, plus the team's real finish.
- **Elite mode** shows names only until the end.
- One Major respin (which also respins the team) and one team respin per game.

Play it: https://kurtid-slop.github.io/major-draft/

The site is a single static page in `docs/` (served by GitHub Pages from that folder). Player stats come from HLTV.
